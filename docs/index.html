Why Sonata

Linux, without the learning curve.

Linux is fast, private and free. What kept people away was everything around it. Sonata takes that part off your hands.

🖱️

Click, don't type

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, displays, sound, printers, users and updates all live in Settings and the Control Center. You never need a terminal.

✨

Beautiful by default

Glass, smooth animations and one consistent look for every app, including Chrome, Steam and the rest. Nothing to set up first.

🧭

Feels familiar

A Dock, a menu bar, a grid of your apps and the Windows shortcuts you already know: Alt+Tab, Super+D, Win+V and more.

🛟

Takes care of itself

If something goes wrong, Sonata starts again on its own and offers to reopen your apps. Updates show up as a notification.

Make it yours

Every detail, your way.

Pick a colour. Go dark. Make the glass clearer or more frosted. Sonata changes with you, live, without a restart.

  • Any accent colour, light or dark, following the time of day if you like
  • Glass and transparency for the Dock, menu bar, menus and windows, each on its own
  • Icon shapes and sizes, corner roundness, Dock size and magnification
  • Your apps as a full-screen grid or a compact Apps Menu
  • Any website as an app of its own, with its own icon and login

Apps included

Everything you need, ready on day one.

Files, notes, calendar, music, a task manager and more, all designed together, all in light and dark.

And more

The things you'd miss, already here.

🎮

Games

Steam games get their own name and icon in the Dock, Alt+Tab and the sound mixer. Game mode kicks in by itself.

🌐

Web apps

Turn WhatsApp, Notion or any site into an app with one right-click: its own window, icon and notifications.

Screenshots and recording

A display, a window or an area, with sound, from one small toolbar.

🖥️

See and control your computer from another device, always with a password.

Report a problem in two clicks

Feedbacker gathers the logs for you and opens a ready-made report.

Get Sonata

One command to install. None after that.

Sonata is in early alpha and works best on Arch-based systems like CachyOS today. Installing still takes one line in a terminal. From then on, everything is a click.

Open a terminal once

Copy the line on the right and paste it in.

Log out

Choose “Sonata” on the login screen.

That's it

A short setup welcomes you, and updates arrive as notifications.

# download Sonata and install it git clone https://github.com/vinioliveiras/sonata2.git cd sonata2 && ./install.sh

Works with Wayfire, GTK 4 and libadwaita on most distributions; the installer gets everything else for you. Found a bug? Tell us on GitHub.
